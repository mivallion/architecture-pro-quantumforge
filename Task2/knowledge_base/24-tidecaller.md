# Tidecaller

The Tidecaller is an resident concerned exclusively with catching fish, who appears as a young child. He does not sell anything, but rather assigns quests to wayfarers. Quest objectives generally involve catching him rare fish from particular biomes or layers, for which he dispenses various rewards.
Additionally, on he will gain various stat increases as the wayfarer progresses.
The Tidecaller is first encountered as the Sleeping Tidecaller randomly spawning on the sand or on the water Overland of the Outer Sea biome (or rarely in the Emberdeep). He will move in when
There is an empty house.
The wayfarer has interacted with him and has woken him up ().
