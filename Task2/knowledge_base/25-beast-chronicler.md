# Beast Chronicler

The Beast Chronicler is an resident vendor that sells animal-related items and vanity. She will move in when the following criteria have been met
There is an empty house.
The Bestiary has been filled to at least 10% ().
She sells a large variety of vanity items, in addition to many mounts and pets, critter-themed accessories, and the Universal Pylon, depending on the Bestiary's completion percentage.
The Beast Chronicler will attack nearby enemies with her claws to defend herself.
