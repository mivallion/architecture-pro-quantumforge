# Reveler

The Reveler is a Founding Era resident vendor who sells festive novelty items and furniture.
Unlike most other town residents, the Reveler has a random chance to spawn every morning and evening once the criteria below are met, rather than being guaranteed to appear once fulfilling them. This random chance also applies to her respawning following her death. Said criteria require that
There is no other resident waiting to move in.
There is an empty house.
14 / 12 / 8 other town residents are in the world. Note that while the Bound Elder, Traveling Quartermaster, and count towards this total, the does not.
