# Arcanist

The Arcanist is a Ascendant Era resident vendor who sells magic-related items. As soon as the Gatekeeper Mass of Flesh has been defeated, he rarely spawns in the cavern layer or below. Similar to the Gearling Artificer, Gearwright, Greensward Adept, Hearth Warden, and Threadshaper, he first spawns as the stationary Bound Arcanist. Interacting with him will free him, after which he will respawn as long as a vacant house is available, even if killed.
He will occasionally replace his regular quote with a comment about the wayfarer's luck. This is the only way to estimate a wayfarer's luck value in the game.
