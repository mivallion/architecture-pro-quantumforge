# Sovereign of the Pale Orbit

The Sovereign of the Pale Orbit is a Ascendant Era, post-Astral Heretic boss. He is the final boss of the versions of Aetherfall.
See below for the Moon Bite debuff inflicted by the Sovereign of the Pale Orbit.

## Behavior

The Sovereign of the Pale Orbit has two hands and a head that must be killed before his core becomes available for attack. He positions his two hands on either side of the wayfarer some distance away.
Each hand, as well as his head, contains a vulnerable eye-like target that is each periodically closed off and opened via an eyelid. These may only be damaged during their "open" states. The core, which is the only part required to be destroyed in order to defeat the boss, only becomes vulnerable when all eye sockets are destroyed.
The Sovereign of the Pale Orbit can teleport to wayfarers if all of them get too far out of range.
Attacks
The Sovereign of the Pale Orbit's hands move away from the core and their eyes spawn six . After a couple seconds, they explode, dealing heavy damage. If the wayfarer is far enough away, they simultaneously charge at the wayfarer before exploding, though they cannot travel at an angle higher than approximately 30 degrees in any direction.
The hand eyes also spawn multiple , which move in all directions for a short while before homing in on the wayfarer. Like the Phantasmal Spheres, they cannot travel at an upward angle, so they can be evaded by swerving upwards. If they cannot get a direct lock and a wayfarer is in range then they will attempt to match the target's trajectory to stay in their path. They explode upon contact with a block or wayfarer.
The hand eyes also occasionally fire two in quick succession, aiming at the wayfarer, which pass through blocks, requiring swerves to avoid. Another viable option is to quickly drop down before the bolts fire, saving the wayfarer from getting hit.
When attached to the Sovereign of the Pale Orbit's head, the head eye opens every 20–21 seconds for a few seconds, and then fires a : a large laser that sweeps 60 degrees upward over the wayfarer. This is by far Sovereign of the Pale Orbit's most damaging attack. It may almost take half of a wayfarer’s HP, making it much harder to survive the proceeding attacks. Afterward, the head eye blinks a couple times, and fires two (similarly to those of the hands), and then closes. As of 1.4, the Head eye will also fire two before performing the Deathray attack, the Deathray can pass through solid blocks as well. In the getfixedboi seed, after the Phantasmal Deathray begins, the Sovereign of the Pale Orbit will eject around 5 s, which bounce about very quickly and over long distances and inflict massive damage upon touching the wayfarer. These can be devastating if the wayfarer is fighting the Sovereign of the Pale Orbit close to the ground due to the Moon Boulders bouncing rapidly and unpredictably off blocks.
After the Deathray attack, the mouth attempts to attach a Tentacle to the wayfarer. The Tentacle can reach through solid blocks. Once attached, it spawns multiple , which are enemies that travel from the wayfarer to the Sovereign of the Pale Orbit's mouth. Any Moon Leech Clot that reaches the mouth without being killed returns 1000 health to one of the Sovereign of the Pale Orbit's parts (one that has not yet been defeated).
The Tentacle also inflicts the Moon Bite debuff, preventing any life-steal effects from weapons/armor like Vampire Knives or Spectre Armor for 14 seconds (16 seconds in Veteran Covenant).
Once the Head eye is defeated, the Tentacle attack becomes more common, with only about a 2 second break between attacks.
When a hand or head target is defeated, it becomes a thorny crevice that deals damage on contact.
Additionally, when a head or hand target is defeated, a spawns, which cycles through the following attacks
The eye fires two at the wayfarer, similar to those from the hand eyes.
The eye spawns six in a hexagonal formation around itself, which after a few seconds will charge directly at the wayfarer, dealing a significant amount of damage to the wayfarer. This is by far the dominant source of damage in the second phase and should be avoided by swerving/dashing.
The eye spawns , which behave similarly to when before the eyes were defeated.
The eye charges and fires a lesser , smaller and dealing significantly less damage. Unlike the primary laser, these attacks cannot go through blocks.
Finally, the eye performs another attack.
The True Eyes of Nythrax are invincible and will only despawn if either all wayfarers die or the Sovereign of the Pale Orbit is defeated. The eyes remain stationary for most of their attacks.
The Sovereign of the Pale Orbit will teleport to the nearest wayfarer in a blinding flash if the wayfarer he is targeting dies or moves more than 150 tiles away.
