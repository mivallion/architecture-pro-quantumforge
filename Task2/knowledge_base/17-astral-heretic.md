# Astral Heretic

The Astral Heretic is a Ascendant Era, post-Sunken Sentinel boss summoned by killing the Astral Acolytes that spawn at the Vault of Echoes's entrance after Sunken Sentinel is defeated. Defeating the Astral Heretic initiates the Astral Convergence.
After all four Celestial Pillars have been destroyed, The Sovereign of the Pale Orbit will spawn and the Astral Acolytes will respawn at the Vault of Echoes entrance, allowing the wayfarer to fight the Astral Heretic and initiate the Astral Convergence again.
Unlike most bosses, the Astral Heretic does not drop any weapons; however, it does drop the Ancient Manipulator, a placeable item that is used to craft many endgame items.

## Behavior

The Astral Heretic makes its first brief appearance at the Mysterious Tablet as soon as the Astral Acolytes are defeated. It is not vulnerable to attack at this time. It then warps to just above where the wayfarer was when the teleport started, and cycles between three attacks
A burst of three (four in Veteran Covenant) that lightly home in on the wayfarer, exploding on impact
A ball of that fires ice shards in six directions four times over as it flies in one direction before slowly fading away
A that fires five bolts of lightning at the character
When the Astral Heretic reaches half health, it will occasionally replace an attack with two spreads of five destructible projectiles.
In Veteran Covenant after reaching half health, it may also replace an attack with numerous that fire four purple projectiles in a cross pattern.
The Astral Heretic cycles through five attacks (six when below half health) before proceeding to spawn phantom duplicates of itself called Ancient Astral Acolytes, along with a larger version of the Mysterious Tablet via a "ritual". Unless the one true Astral Heretic is interrupted by an attack from a wayfarer during this ritual, the duplicates fly in formation and add their homing attacks.
Attacking a duplicate or taking too long hitting the real Astral Acolyte results in the summoning of the Wyvern-like . If there is already a Phantasm Dragon inbound, attacking a duplicate triggers an , which will charge the wayfarer diagonally. Duplicates can be eliminated by hitting the true Astral Heretic during the ritual, but if not, large quantities of duplicates can amass.
