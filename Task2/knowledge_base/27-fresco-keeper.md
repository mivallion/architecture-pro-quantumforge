# Fresco Keeper

The Fresco Keeper is an resident vendor who sells and painting tools, as well as paintings and . He will spawn once the following conditions are met
There is an empty house.
8 / 8 other town residents are in the world. Note that while the Bound Elder, Traveling Quartermaster, and count towards this total, the does not.
The Fresco Keeper's shop inventory changes based on external factors. The biome in which he is talked to, the moon phase, and seasonal events all determine which paintings and wallpapers are available. As of version 1.4.4, wallpapers and paintings are sold under the interaction labelled "Decor" while paints, paint tools, and pylons are sold under the normal "Shop" option.
