# Mirrorborn Pair

Mirrorborn Pair are a Ascendant Era mechanical boss and are essentially a more difficult version of the Watcher of the Rift. Mirrorborn Pair are two separate flying entities, Retinazer and Spazmatism, connected by a tendril, each with its own attack pattern and life count. Both must be killed in order to defeat the boss.
Mirrorborn Pair must be killed before dawn, otherwise they flee and despawn.
