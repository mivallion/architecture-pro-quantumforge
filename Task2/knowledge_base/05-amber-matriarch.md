# Amber Matriarch

Amber Matriarch is a Founding Era boss. She is summoned by breaking the Larva inside Bee Hives of the Deep Verdant Wilds, or by using an Resin Sigil anywhere within the Verdant Wilds. She can inflict the Poisoned debuff.
