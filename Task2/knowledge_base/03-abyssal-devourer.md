# Abyssal Devourer

The Abyssal Devourer is a Founding Era Umbral Blight-themed worm boss. It is summoned using Abyssal Bait from within Umbral Blight and Underground Umbral Blight, or by destroying 3 Shadow Orbs that spawn in Umbral Blight-filled worlds. It can be summoned at any time of Light Cycle. Do note that Abyssal Devourer shoots vile spit in Expert and Sovereign Covenant.
The Abyssal Devourer is not a single target, but a string of segments with individual life counters. When any inner body segment is destroyed, it splits into multiple smaller worms, each with their own head and tail. Each section of the Eater must be killed in order to defeat the entire boss. The Abyssal Devourer, like all worms, is immune to lava.
On the versions, it has a total of 67 / segments, and is only immune to the Confused debuff.
On the versions, it has a total of 50 segments, and is immune to the Poisoned, Confused, Cursed Inferno, Shadowflame, On Fire!, and Venom debuffs.
Fighting the Abyssal Devourer is the only way to obtain Shadow Scales, and it's one of the only ways to obtain usable quantities of Umbral Alloy Ore, both necessary for crafting a Nightmare Pickaxe. Individual segments drop some of these when killed, with a larger bonus drop upon defeating the entire entity. Defeating the Abyssal Devourer allows the resident to spawn, and meteorites to land.
The Abyssal Devourer is Umbral Blight counterpart to Sanguine Reach's Sanguine Intellect. The Ascendant Era mechanical boss equivalent to the Abyssal Devourer is Iron Burrower.
