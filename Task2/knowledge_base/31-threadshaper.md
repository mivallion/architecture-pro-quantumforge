# Threadshaper

The Threadshaper is an resident that can change the wayfarer's hair style and color. She also sells a variety of Hair Dyes, which apply dynamic effects to the wayfarer's hair.
The Threadshaper can be found in Spider Caves, in the Cavern layer, entangled in cobweb. Talking to her will free her, after which she will move into a vacant house if one is available. As with all found/bound residents, she spawns the same as enemies.
