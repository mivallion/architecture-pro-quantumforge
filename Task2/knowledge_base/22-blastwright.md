# Blastwright

The Blastwright is an resident vendor that will spawn once the following criteria have been met
There is an empty house.
The wayfarer has an explosive in the inventory.
The Quartermaster is present.
If the Blastwright is killed, he will only respawn if the above conditions are met again. This means an explosive must be placed in the wayfarer's inventory again. Having explosives stored in chests or other containers will also fulfill the requirement.
Moreover, on the , residents including the Blastwright will gain various stat increases as the wayfarer progresses.
