# Prismatic Regent

Prismatic Regent is an early-Ascendant Era, Luminous Expanse-themed boss. Her appearance is that of a massive, translucent pink Gelkin wearing a silver crown and engulfing a colorful crystal. Fighting Prismatic Regent is completely optional and is not required for game progression—much like her Founding Era counterpart, Gelid Regent—but her defeat may reward the wayfarer with useful items.

## Behavior
