# Ossuary Warden

Ossuary Warden is a Founding Era boss. It must be defeated prior to entering the Vault of Echoes, and defeating it grants unlimited access to all Vault of Echoes areas for all wayfarers. It also causes the Clothier resident to spawn, as long as a vacant house is available.

## Behavior

Ossuary Warden is a large floating skull with two independent floating hands. All parts can travel through blocks, and will attempt to make contact with the wayfarer to inflict damage. Each part can be killed independently, but killing the head is all that is required to defeat Ossuary Warden.
Ossuary Warden alternates between two attack phases
The head remains above while the hands slash at the wayfarer.
The head spins, dealing significant damage if touched, but dropping its defense by 10 (to in Standard Covenant) for the duration of the attack.
Ossuary Warden switches between these two phases in a regular cycle during the entire fight, with the first phase lasting for roughly 13.33 seconds and the second phase lasting for roughly 6.67 seconds. It lets out a every time it enters the second phase.
If the wayfarer takes too long to kill it and dawn arrives, Ossuary Warden will begin acting exactly as a Vault of Echoes Guardian, performing a head spin attack with very high damage and gaining enough defense to only absorb 1 damage from all attacks, which will usually kill any wayfarers remaining in the vicinity quickly (it is still possible to kill it, but wayfarers are at an extreme disadvantage unless it only has a miniscule amount of health). Ossuary Warden remains in this form until dying or despawning, even if wayfarers manage to avoid dying until the following dusk.
