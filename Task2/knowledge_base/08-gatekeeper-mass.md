# Gatekeeper Mass

the Gatekeeper Mass of Flesh is the final and strongest Founding Era boss, spawnable only in The Emberdeep. Once it is defeated, the world permanently converts to Ascendant Era, which brings new content and challenges. This means that once the Gatekeeper Mass is defeated, new biomes will spawn, new monsters will attack you, and the game can be beaten.
To defeat the Gatekeeper Mass of Flesh, the wayfarer must attack either its eyes or its mouth. They all share the same life counter, so attacking any of the two will deplete the total health of the boss. However, the eyes have less defense (thus take more damage) than the mouth. No other parts of the Gatekeeper Mass can be damaged.
See below for information on the two exclusive debuffs from the boss, Horrified and The Tongue.

## Aftermath

When the Gatekeeper Mass of Flesh is killed, it leaves its drops in a midair frame of Umbral Alloy Bricks for Umbral Blight worlds, or for Sanguine Reach worlds. It will always drop the Rift Maul, which is required to break Ritual Forges and generate Ascendant Era ores in the world.
The Ember Core can only be obtained and used once. If the wayfarer has already consumed one, additional Treasure Bags will not yield additional Demon Hearts. If the wayfarer somehow obtains a second Ember Core, i.e. from another wayfarer or from researching in Archivist Covenant, they will be unable to consume it.
Ascendant Era
Two diagonal stripes of Luminous Expanse and Umbral Blight or Sanguine Reach form in a "V" pattern from the center of the Emberdeep, all the way to the Overland. These biomes will also begin to spread to more blocks and spread at a much faster rate, eventually covering large areas of the world if no action is taken.
