# Iron Burrower

Iron Burrower is a Ascendant Era mechanical boss which is essentially a more difficult version of the Abyssal Devourer. Like the Abyssal Devourer, it is a very large worm. Although it appears to be similarly made up of many segments, individual segments cannot die, and Iron Burrower will not split apart into multiple entities; its segments share a singular health pool. Like other worms, Iron Burrower takes increased damage from attacks that damage multiple segments at once.
Each of Iron Burrower's segments fires lasers at the wayfarer. The red-lit segments contain a , which is an independent flying enemy that is launched periodically as the boss takes damage and also fires lasers. Probes can be killed individually once detached, and never return to Iron Burrower.
Iron Burrower must be killed before dawn, otherwise it flees and despawns.

## Behavior

Iron Burrower spawns coiled up, like most worm enemies, and immediately uncoils and moves toward the wayfarer. It follows the wayfarer from any distance, and will only despawn if the wayfarer dies or if dawn comes (4:30AM). It quickly travels below the ground until it is off-screen, then despawns.
Iron Burrower leaps from the ground and attempts to ram the wayfarer. It has limited mobility when fully in the air, requiring blocks or platforms to "burrow" through. It also attempts to surround the wayfarer, and if the wayfarer is too close to the ground it can usually succeed.
In addition, Iron Burrower performs the following attacks
A segment has a chance to release a Probe whenever it is damaged, but once a segment releases a probe, it cannot do it again. The red light goes out when it releases the Probe. Probes will fly towards the wayfarer, attempting to maintain a distance while occasionally moving into melee range. They fire with moderate accuracy, able to reliably hit a stationary wayfarer. Probes do not die when the boss dies, so they may have to be killed even after Iron Burrower has been defeated.
