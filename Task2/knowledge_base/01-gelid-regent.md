# Gelid Regent

Gelid Regent is a Founding Era boss. His appearance is that of a massive Blue Gelkin wearing a jeweled golden crown and having what appears to be a ninja inside of him. He lacks the crown on the versions of the game. As Gelid Regent takes damage, he spawns additional ordinary Blue Slimes (and in Expert and Sovereign Covenant) and will also appear to shrink in size.
Gelid Regent spawns under one of the following conditions
A wayfarer uses a Gelkin Crown.
150 slimes are defeated during the Gelkin Rain event (or 75 if Gelid Regent was already slain in the world at least once).
Rarely in the outer lateral sixths of the map, during daytime, and only on grass.
Fighting Gelid Regent is completely optional and not required for game progression, but his defeat may reward the wayfarer with money and useful items.
