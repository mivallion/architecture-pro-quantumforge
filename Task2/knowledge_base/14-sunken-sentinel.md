# Sunken Sentinel

Sunken Sentinel is a Ascendant Era, post-Thornheart boss found in the Rootbound Sanctum and is by far one of the easiest bosses in the game relative to game progression.

## Behavior

While Sunken Sentinel's main body can not normally pass through blocks, its fists, lasers, and head (when detached in the second phase) can. On the , if the body's path to the wayfarer is blocked, it will leap on the wayfarer, allowing it to pass through all barriers (similar to the behavior of a biome mimic).
First form
Sunken Sentinel shoots ricocheting fireballs, attacks with its unstoppable fists that extend from chains, and jumps around. These increase in frequency as each part takes more damage. Sunken Sentinel's fists can travel through blocks, but have limited range. In addition, his fists will also glow when they are about to extend—they will extend 0.5 seconds after glowing. Sunken Sentinel jumps around more often when a fist is destroyed. This compounds when both fists are destroyed.
Second form
When Sunken Sentinel's stone head has taken full damage, it detaches itself and becomes invincible, capable of passing through blocks. Its body ( health) then becomes the target. If the fists have not been destroyed, they will continue to attack the wayfarer more rapidly while the body continues to jump around. The head follows same attack pattern as before and will increase in frequency when the body is damaged. The only way to defeat Sunken Sentinel at this point is to completely destroy his body, which will also destroy his head, ending the fight.
Enraged behavior
Unlike Thornheart, Sunken Sentinel's enraged behavior is not noticeable until reaching the end of its first form, when it begins jumping around and firing lasers at a faster rate. Furthermore, the difference between his enraged and normal behavior will not be noticeable to most without deliberately switching between the two.

## Aftermath

Once Sunken Sentinel is defeated
Astral Acolytes spawn at the Vault of Echoes's entrance which when killed, summons the Astral Heretic and then the Astral Convergence (as long as certain other conditions are also met – see details).
For this to happen, Ossuary Warden must have been defeated at least once - the Bound Elder spawning will override the Astral Acolytes' spawning.
Likewise, Sovereign of the Pale Orbit can be summoned directly via the Astral Seal.
Martian Probes begin spawning randomly, which can trigger the Skyforged Incursion event.
Summoning the Ancestor Legion after Sunken Sentinel has been defeated triggers the most difficult "Tier 3" version of the event enabling you to fight Betsy.
The Steampunker will begin to sell the Steampunk Wings.
