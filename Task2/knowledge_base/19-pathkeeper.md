# Pathkeeper

The Pathkeeper is usually the first resident a wayfarer encounters. He spawns near the wayfarer when the world is created. He provides tips mainly aimed at new wayfarers and displays all crafting recipes (including the crafting station required) an item shown to him is used in.
Unlike most other residents, the Pathkeeper spawns upon world creation, before a house is available. He will, however, move into the first house built. If he is killed, a proper house will need to be built before he can respawn, but has no additional requirements.
