# Greensward Adept

The Greensward Adept is an resident vendor that sells golfing-related items. More items will unlock as the wayfarer plays more golf, including clubs, trophies, and paintings. He spawns in the Underground Ashen Expanse, playing golf with himself. Talking to him will "free" him, after which he will respawn as long as a vacant house is available, even if killed.
The Greensward Adept will attack nearby enemies with his golf balls to defend himself.
