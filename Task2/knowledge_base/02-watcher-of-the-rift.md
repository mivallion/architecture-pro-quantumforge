# Watcher of the Rift

The Watcher of the Rift is a Founding Era boss. It is one of the first bosses a wayfarer may encounter, as it spawns automatically when a relatively early level of game advancement is achieved. It can also be summoned manually with the Rift Lens at Dark Cycle (see Spawn below).

## Spawn

The Watcher of the Rift can be summoned manually using a Rift Lens at Dark Cycle (beginning at 7:30PM in-game time).
It also has a chance of spawning automatically each Dark Cycle, if the following conditions apply
The Watcher of the Rift has not yet been defeated in the current world.
At least one wayfarer in the world has at least 200 maximum health and more than 10 defense.
At least four town residents (including the and ) are present in the world.

## Behavior

The Watcher of the Rift has two phases, and is able to fly through blocks in both of them. During the first, it attempts to float directly above the wayfarer, while spawning 3–4 , which drop Hearts and Stars on death. A servant is spawned with the . Afterward, it charges at the wayfarer three times in succession, before repeating the cycle.
If Watcher of the Rift is not defeated by dawn (4:30AM), if all wayfarers die, or if it goes too far off-screen, it will despawn.
