# Sanguine Intellect

The Sanguine Intellect is a Founding Era, Sanguine Reach-themed boss resembling a large, flying brain. It can be summoned when 3 Sanguine Reach Hearts are destroyed in a Sanguine Reach world, or when a wayfarer uses a Sanguine Vertebra anywhere in Sanguine Reach or Underground Sanguine Reach biome.
The Sanguine Intellect has two stages. In the first, the Brain is invulnerable and teleports randomly around the wayfarer. 20 Creepers spawn along with the Brain and orbit the boss at high speed. Creepers do not respawn once they are killed. All Creepers must be destroyed first before the Sanguine Intellect enters its second phase, where it becomes vulnerable to attack and can be killed.
Defeating the Sanguine Intellect and its attendant Creepers is the only way to obtain Tissue Samples, and is one of the ways to obtain usable quantities of Sanguine Alloy Ore. Defeating the Sanguine Intellect for the first time allows the Hearth Warden resident to spawn.
The Sanguine Intellect is Sanguine Reach counterpart to the Abyssal Devourer. Unlike its Umbral Blight counterpart, it does not have a mechanical equivalent in Ascendant Era.
