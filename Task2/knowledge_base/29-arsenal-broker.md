# Arsenal Broker

The Arsenal Broker is an resident that will spawn once the following criteria have been met
There is an empty house.
The wayfarer has at least one bullet or bullet-firing gun in their inventory (guns stored in storage items like chests do not count).
If the Arsenal Broker is killed, he will only respawn if there's an available house in the town.
As wayfarers progress, he will gain various stat increases.
