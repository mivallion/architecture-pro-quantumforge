# Frostbound Colossus

Frostbound Colossus is a Founding Era , encountered in the Snow biome at midnight during a Blizzard if at least one wayfarer in the world has either at least 9 defense or 200 health, or by summoning it using a Deer Thing in the Snow biome. It will not spawn naturally if there are residents too close to the wayfarer. Fighting the Frostbound Colossus is not required for game progression, but defeating it rewards the wayfarer with coins and other useful items.

## Behavior

The Frostbound Colossus has four attacks (five in Expert or higher difficulties.)
If it is close to the wayfarer, the Frostbound Colossus will smash the ground, summoning a wave of ice spikes that quickly travels towards them. Every third consecutive wave does not go as far, instead spreading in both directions.
If it is not close enough to its target to use the ice spikes attack, the Frostbound Colossus will 'scoop' and then throw numerous chunks of debris into the air that fall back down. These chunks can pass through blocks.
If the wayfarer is above the Frostbound Colossus for enough time, five shadow hands will be summoned around the wayfarer.
In Veteran Covenant, shadow hands will be periodically summoned that either move forwards slowly, move forwards quickly and curve, or spin in place. Shadow hands are summoned more frequently as the Frostbound Colossus' life gets lower.
The Frostbound Colossus cannot pass through solid blocks, but if the wayfarer is above it, far enough away, and the Frostbound Colossus cannot reach them, it will quickly rise through the blocks to reach the wayfarer.
If the wayfarer is more than 30 tiles away, the Frostbound Colossus will turn a dark shade purple, two reddish, semi transparent Frostbound Colossus' will begin spinning around it, and it becomes immune to all damage.
If all wayfarers are dead or there are none nearby when it spawned naturally, the Frostbound Colossus will wander around instead of immediately despawning. This will happen until a wayfarer gets close to the boss, at which point the fight will resume as normal. It will despawn after a few minutes have passed while wayfarers are not near or upon leaving and re-entering the world.
Like all other bosses, Pylons cannot be used while the Frostbound Colossus is alive in a world.
