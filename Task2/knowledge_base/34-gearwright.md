# Gearwright

The Gearwright is an resident who sells various mechanisms, as well as Wire Cutters and Wrenches, allowing the wayfarer to construct and activate wired devices like traps and critter engines. She can be found in the Vault of Echoes in the Cavern layer or below after killing Ossuary Warden, where she spawns randomly as the stationary Bound Gearwright. Talking to her in this state will free her, after which she will respawn as long as a vacant house is available, even if killed.
When submerged in Fluxwater, the Gearwright will transform into her Shimmered form. This has no impact on gameplay or dialogue and is only a visual change. She can be returned to her classic look by submerging her in Fluxwater again.
