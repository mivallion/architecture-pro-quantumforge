# Radiant Monarch

The Radiant Monarch is a Ascendant Era, post-Thornheart boss fought in Luminous Expanse. While she can be fought as soon as Thornheart is defeated, she is significantly more difficult than most Ascendant Era bosses and will typically require post-Sunken Sentinel gear. The Radiant Monarch fight is optional and is not required for progression. Her appearance is that of a yellow-skinned fairy with bioluminescent wings, a crown and dress.

## Behavior

If fought during the Light Cycle, the Radiant Monarch enters an "enraged" state, indicated by the color of attacks turning a bright yellow. While enraged, all of her attacks instantly kill the wayfarer regardless of equipment.
See below for exceptions.
If summoned during the Light Cycle and not defeated in time, she will vanish at 7:30PM and drop nothing.
If fought in Expert or Sovereign Covenant, she will always use her enraged attack pattern, but without the massive damage increase.
Attacks
Aurora Shards - Summons a cloud of Aurora Shards that home in on the wayfarer after a short delay. These bolts do not have perfect tracking, and will miss if the wayfarer makes a sharp turn as they approach.
Aurora Shards 2 - Fires Aurora Shards in a clockwise spiral, which behave identically to those used in the first variant of the attack. This version is harder to dodge, and when she is enraged she summons more bolts.
Dash Attack - Positions herself to the left or right of the wayfarer before charging horizontally in the wayfarer’s direction. This attack is additionally preceded by a sound effect and several images of the Radiant Monarch spinning around her right before she dashes. She briefly becomes invincible during the attack, removing any Daybreak spears attached to her and marks from whips.
Sun Dance - Hovers above and slightly to the left of the wayfarer and emits 6 persistent rays of light (8 if enraged) that slowly rotate clockwise around her. 3 sets of rays are created per sun dance, each positioned slightly further clockwise than the last.
Endless Spectrum - Releases a ring of 13 stars that slowly spiral out clockwise from her original position, then spiral back inward in the same direction. These stars leave behind a trail of light that will damage the wayfarer.
Aether Spear - Summons a swarm of swords that launch in the direction the wayfarer was headed when they were summoned, telegraphed by colored beams. These swords are summoned behind where the wayfarer was moving, or all around the wayfarer if they were stationary. The swords do no damage until launched. If enraged, extra swords are summoned, and they will launch faster.
Aether Spear 2 - Summons four evenly spaced waves of swords that travel across the screen, telegraphed by colored beams. The first wave travels from the left side of the screen to the right, the second from right to left, the third wave from top-left to bottom-right, and the fourth wave from top-right to bottom-left.
If enraged, there will instead be six waves of swords that target specific points around the wayfarer instead of traveling from one side of the screen to the other. The first wave travels from the left side of the screen to the right of the wayfarer, the second wave from the right side to the left of the wayfarer, the third wave from top side to the bottom-right of the wayfarer, the fourth wave from top side to bottom-left of the wayfarer, the fifth wave from the bottom side to top-right of the wayfarer, and the sixth wave from the bottom side to top left of the wayfarer.
Attack Pattern
The Radiant Monarch has set attack patterns for both of her phases.
First Phase
When spawned, the Radiant Monarch will hover in place for a few seconds before executing the following attacks in order
Aurora Shards
Dash Attack
Sun Dance
Dash Attack
Endless Spectrum
Aurora Shards
Dash Attack
Aether Spear
Dash Attack
Endless Spectrum
This sequence of attacks repeats indefinitely until the second phase starts.
Second Phase
When the Radiant Monarch is below half of her max health, she will disappear for a second then reappear above the next estimated location of the wayfarer, adopting a new appearance before executing a new set of attacks, with slightly shorter attack intervals than before
Aether Spear 2
Aurora Shards
Dash Attack
If enraged, she will cast a modified version of Aether Spear after the dash before resuming her normal pattern (targets where the wayfarer is rather than where they will be)
Endless Spectrum
Aurora Shards
Sun Dance
Aether Spear
Dash Attack
Aurora Shards 2
This sequence of attacks repeats indefinitely until she is defeated.
