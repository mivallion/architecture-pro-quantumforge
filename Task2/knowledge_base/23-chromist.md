# Chromist

The Chromist is an resident that sells the Dye Vat and other dye-related items. He will move in when the following criteria have been met
There is an empty house.
The wayfarer has a dye, dye ingredient, or Strange Plant in their inventory, and at least four other town residents are in the world. Note that while the Bound Elder, Traveling Quartermaster, and town pets count towards this total, the Skeleton Quartermaster does not.
One of the following
The wayfarer has a Strange Plant in their inventory.
The wayfarer has a dye or dye ingredient in their inventory, and one of the four major Founding Era bosses has been defeated: Watcher of the Rift, Ossuary Warden, or Abyssal Devourer / Sanguine Intellect depending on the world's evil biome.
The wayfarer has a dye or dye ingredient in their inventory.
In addition to selling dye-related items, wayfarers may trade in Strange Plants for uncraftable and unpurchasable animated dyes once Ascendant Era has been reached. These dyes are given at random in sets of six / three for each exchanged Strange Plant.
