# Gilded Bone Engine

Gilded Bone Engine is a Ascendant Era mechanical boss which is essentially a more difficult version of Ossuary Warden. It consists of a head and four limbs, but, similarly to Ossuary Warden, only the head needs to be defeated to defeat the boss.
If Gilded Bone Engine is not defeated by dawn, it will behave much like a Vault of Echoes Guardian: It will cease using its limbs and instead charge at the wayfarer at extremely high speeds, dealing usually lethal damage upon contact.

## Behavior

Gilded Bone Engine's head constantly endeavors to remain between 31.5 and 12.5 tiles above the wayfarer and within 6.25 tiles of either side of the wayfarer. It can accelerate infinitely in order to move back into this area. The head does not actively attempt to make contact with the wayfarer.
Gilded Bone Engine's four limbs all have unique attacks
Prime Cannon fires every 2.33 seconds that do not destroy tiles, usually up into the air rather than directly at the wayfarer. It usually stays above the wayfarer.
Prime Saw usually remains under the wayfarer, moving up and down.
Prime Vice will swipe around wildly, making occasional lunges at the wayfarer. It usually remains diagonal to the wayfarer.
Prime Laser shoots red Death Lasers every 3.33 seconds, similar to those of Retinazer and Iron Burrower, and is always pointed at a wayfarer. It usually stays horizontal to the wayfarer.
Every 10 seconds, Gilded Bone Engine will enter a spinning phase for 6.67 seconds, in which its attacks become more aggressive
Prime Cannon will aim at the wayfarer directly and decrease its cooldown between firing to 0.67 seconds.
Prime Saw will begin homing directly towards the wayfarer's location.
Prime Vice becomes more aggressive and frequently lunges towards the wayfarer.
Prime Laser will fire lasers every 1.33 seconds.
Gilded Bone Engine lets out a every time it enters the spinning phase.
Upon dawn (4:30AM), Gilded Bone Engine will enrage and permanently behave like a faster Vault of Echoes Guardian, increasing its velocity and usually killing the wayfarer instantly if touched: its damage increases to 1000 and its defense raises up to 9999. It will remain in this state until defeated, even if it becomes Dark Cycle again. If defeated, it will drop items normally.
If the wayfarer is more than 375 tiles away, Gilded Bone Engine will start moving downwards and will quickly despawn.
