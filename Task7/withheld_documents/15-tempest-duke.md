# Tempest Duke

Tempest Duke is a Ascendant Era boss. While he can be summoned as soon as Ascendant Era is initiated, he can pose a serious threat to new or underprepared wayfarers and should be fought later on in the game. Some wayfarers may challenge him with post-mechanical bosses gear, but most wayfarers may want to wait until post-Sunken Sentinel to battle him. Fighting Tempest Duke is completely optional and not required for , but his defeat will reward the wayfarer with powerful weapons.
