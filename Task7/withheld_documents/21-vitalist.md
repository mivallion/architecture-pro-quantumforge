# Vitalist

The Vitalist is an resident that will spawn once the following criteria have been met
There is an empty house.
The wayfarer's maximum health is greater than 100.
The Quartermaster is present.
The Vitalist will heal the wayfarer's health and cure them of any active debuffs at the cost of Coins (there are exceptions, see Notes and History).
In the versions, healing costs can range from a few Copper to several Silver Coins, depending on how much health the wayfarer is missing. She charges Copper equal to 75% of the health restored (e.g. for restoring 100 health), plus an additional for every active debuff on the wayfarer.
